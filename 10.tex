YourStuddybuddyinNeed
We will create an app that helps people organize their task and homework.

Inspiration: We were motivated by the observation that many individuals struggle with procrastination, so developing an app to remind users when to complete their homework could be very beneficial.
What it does: Users input their homework assignments, and the app creates a structured schedule with designated times for each task.
How we built it: We developed the app using Java.
Challenges we encountered: Our code initially did not function as intended, which required us to restart the development process and work on making our solution unique.
Accomplishments we are proud of: We successfully completed the development of the app.
What we learned: We gained insights into effective study tools and improved our organizational skills.
Next steps for Studdybuddy: We are considering adding features that promote positive habits and motivation.
