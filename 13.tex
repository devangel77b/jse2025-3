HobbyHaven
a social media app that allows you to organize and join events with people that have the same hobby.

Inspiration
A desire to find peers easily who have the same interests as you do is something we though many struggle with in our day.

What it does
Allows people to create and join events about their hobby with people of their own age

How we built it
We used multiple languages, including CSS, HTML, and JS. We used multiple tutorials from the website W3 Schools.

Challenges we ran into
We unfortunately could not finish the backend portion of our app, essentially leading to the malfunction of some functions that we intended for the app, including the entire left side panel.

Accomplishments that we're proud of
We are proud of our minimalistic UI and Logo that we made for the app.

What we learned
Upon learning the basics of CSS, HTML, and JS, we learned the basics of Web Development.

What's next for HobbyHaven
We hope to continue this project by adding functionality to all points of the app
